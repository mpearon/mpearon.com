00 Rolodex

The Bond dossier · 1952 — present

The Bond dossier — films, books, games and a scrolling timeline of the franchise.

What it does

TimelineA scrolling timeline of film releases and franchise news, with filters for Films, Books, Games and Other.
Every filmEvery Eon film, plus the non-Eon films, clearly labelled as such.
Fleming's booksIan Fleming's novels and story collections.
Film pagesCast, cars, gadgets and locations for each film.
RatingsYour own ratings, and a watched tracker.

Quick fixes

Posters, covers or photos aren't showing

Artwork is fetched from other services (TMDB, Open Library and Wikimedia Commons), so you need to be online. If one of them is slow or has no image for a title, that picture can be missing.

The timeline's news isn't updating

The app downloads a small news list from mpearon.com. Check your connection and reopen the app. You can see the list yourself at news.json.

Movie-night reminders

Reminders are optional local notifications made on your device. If they don't appear, check that notifications are allowed for the app in iPhone Settings.

Exporting the watch plan to my calendar

The export is a calendar file that you share yourself, for example to your calendar app. The app doesn't add events on its own.

Where are my ratings and watched films kept?

Ratings, watched films, your watch plan and settings are stored on your device only. There are no accounts and nothing is synced.

Is this an official Bond app?

No. It's an unofficial fan project and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Eon Productions, Danjaq, Amazon MGM Studios or the estate of Ian Fleming.

What the app stores

Your ratings, watched films, watch plan and settings are stored on your device only. The app has no accounts and no server of its own that holds your data.

Requests to other services

To show artwork and information, the app sends search requests (film and book titles and years, and search terms) to these third-party services:

  • The Movie Database (TMDB) for film posters, stills, cast, and runtime and budget figures. Privacy policy
  • Open Library for book covers. Privacy policy
  • Wikimedia Commons for photos of cars, gadgets and places. Privacy policy
  • Apple's iTunes Search API for 30-second theme-song previews. Privacy policy

The app also downloads a small news list from mpearon.com (https://mpearon.com/app/00rolodex/news.json).

As with any web request, these services receive the request and your device's IP address. They handle that under their own privacy policies, linked above.

Notifications

Movie-night reminders are optional local notifications. They are generated on your device, and no data leaves the device for them.

Calendar export

The calendar export is a file that you share yourself. Where it goes is up to you.

Selling and sharing

The app itself doesn't sell or share any personal data.
