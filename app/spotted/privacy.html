Spotted

Privacy policy

Spotted does not collect, store or share your personal information. Everything you enter, such as the races you record, your counts, race names and distances, stays on your iPhone.

Spotted has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use the network.

Spotted's widget and Lock Screen counter read the same data on your device so they can show your count. That data never leaves your phone.

If you send feedback through TestFlight, Apple handles it under Apple's own privacy policy.

If you delete the app, the races stored on your phone are deleted with it.
