Spotted

Measure your run in mutts, not minutes. Here's how to get help, and exactly what the app does (and doesn't do) with your data.

Quick fixes

My count isn't showing on the widget or Lock Screen

Open Spotted once; the widget and Live Activity refresh when the app comes to the front. Counts made with the Action Button or the widget are saved on your iPhone straight away, so nothing is lost. If a Live Activity is stuck, finish or cancel the run in the app.

Set up the Action Button

In the iPhone Settings app, open Action Button, swipe to Controls, then choose Spotted's Count. Press and hold it to add one dog, no looking required. Siri works too: say "Count with Spotted".

Spotted doesn't suggest a distance

The distance suggestion comes from your step count, so it needs Motion & Fitness access. Turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Motion & Fitness → Spotted. You can always type a distance yourself, and a suggestion is never saved unless you confirm it.

I forgot to start a run, or need to fix one

Tap the nose and a run starts on its own. Hit a wrong count? Undo removes the last one. For a run you didn't track live, open Past runs from the menu and tap +, then enter the date, count and distance.

Leaderboards won't load

Leaderboards use Game Center, so you need to be signed in to it (Settings → Game Center) and online. They're off until you turn them on in the app. Scores wait in a small queue and send once your phone is back online.

New iPhone, or my runs disappeared

Your runs live on your iPhone, not on a server. They come along when you restore from an iCloud or computer backup or use Quick Start to set up a new phone. If you delete Spotted, its runs are deleted with it, and I can't recover them.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app, and everything stored on your iPhone goes with it. If you turned on Leaderboards, scores stay in Apple's Game Center; Apple controls those, and you can manage them in Settings → Game Center.

What Spotted stores

Spotted keeps your runs, counts (each with a timestamp), run names and distances. All of it lives on your iPhone. I never receive it, and I have no server to send it to. The widget, Lock Screen counter, Live Activity and Action Button read the same on-device data so they can show and update your count. It doesn't leave your phone for that either.

Permissions

Motion & Fitness

If you allow it, Spotted reads your step count for the length of a run to suggest a distance and to add a step count to the text you share. The step count is used on your iPhone and isn't stored or sent anywhere by Spotted. Say no and everything else works.

Leaderboards (optional)

Leaderboards are off by default. If you turn them on, Spotted signs in with Apple's Game Center and sends your scores (for example, most dogs in a run) to it, so friends can compare. That goes through Apple, under Apple's privacy policy. I don't see it or run any server for it. Spotted never sends run names, locations or timestamps.

Sharing a run

The Share button makes a small image and a line of text and hands them to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes is up to you, and Spotted doesn't keep a copy.

Feedback through TestFlight

If you send feedback from a TestFlight beta, Apple handles it under Apple's own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Delete the app and its runs are deleted with it. Any Leaderboards scores in Game Center are managed by Apple.

Children

Spotted doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.
